# stroke_fx

Effects along the strokes of handwritten text, and on the joints where they meet, as it's written: in Latin,
Japanese and Arabic, for Flutter, on [fountain_pen](https://pub.dev/packages/fountain_pen).

<p align="center">
  <b><a href="https://rahiche.github.io/stroke_fx/">Try it in your browser</a></b> ·
  <a href="https://github.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/blob/main/doc/getting_started.md">Get started</a> ·
  <a href="https://github.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/blob/main/doc/how_it_works.md">How it works</a>
</p>

**Neon**: each stroke lights as the pen reaches it.

!['Stroke by stroke' written in neon tubes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/neon_latin.gif)

**Frost**: the ink freezes as it is written.

![Japanese freezing into ice as it is written](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/frost_japanese.gif)

**Sakura**: blossoms open where strokes meet.

![Japanese written as cherry branches that blossom](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/sakura_japanese.gif)

**Ivy**: a vine climbs every stroke.

!['Stroke by stroke' with ivy climbing the strokes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/ivy_latin.gif)

**Fireflies**: fireflies rise from the ink and blink in step.

![Arabic glowing, with fireflies rising from it](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/fireflies_arabic.gif)

**Weld**: every joint sparks as the pen reaches it.

![Japanese welded in steel, sparks at the joints](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/weld_japanese.gif)

**Constellation**: every joint a star.

![Arabic drawn as a constellation, stars at the joints](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/constellation_arabic.gif)

**Snow**: snow settles on every edge that faces up.

![Japanese with snow settling on its upper edges](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/snow_japanese.gif)

**Ember**: molten where the pen just was, cooling.

![Arabic written in cooling molten metal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/ember_arabic.gif)

**Jelly**: the pen drags the jelly; it springs back.

!['Stroke by stroke' in soft wobbling jelly](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/jelly_latin.gif)

**Dew**: silk pinned at its joints sags and gathers dew.

!['Stroke by stroke' as spider silk with dew](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/dew_latin.gif)

**Ink bleed**: wet ink spreads where the pen lingered.

![Japanese ink bleeding into paper](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/ink_bleed_japanese.gif)

**Ribbon**: each stroke a twisting ribbon.

!['Stroke by stroke' as twisting ribbons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/ribbon_latin.gif)

**Plain ink**: fountain_pen's own ink, for comparison.

![Arabic written in plain ink](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/main/doc/plain_ink_arabic.gif)

Every effect runs at 60 fps on macOS and the web, and writes as you type. [Apache 2.0](https://github.com/Rahiche/stroke_fx/blob/main/LICENSE).
