## 0.1.0

- First release: effects that live along the strokes of text written by fountain_pen, and on the joints where
  strokes meet, in Latin, Japanese and Arabic.
- Fourteen effects: Plain ink, Neon, Ember, Ink bleed, Ribbon, Weld, Constellation, Frost, Dew, Sakura, Ivy, Jelly,
  Snow and Fireflies.
- `StrokeFxText`, a drop-in widget; `StrokeText` and `StrokeFxPainter` to paint any moment of the writing yourself;
  `StrokeEffect` and `StrokeShaders.register` for your own effects.
- Writing as it's typed: `WrittenText(typing: true, typedAt: ...)` keeps what's written as it was.
